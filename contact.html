If you still have a question about Kharkiv, you can ask me here. You are not writing to a city portal or an automated system — I lived in Kharkiv, so I will answer from my own experience and try to help with things that are difficult to find in a travel guide or on Wikipedia.

What you can ask

You can ask me about places in Kharkiv, studying there, everyday life, food or other things I can answer from my own experience.

I probably cannot help with visas, current ticket prices, schedules or what is happening in the city right now, because this information can change quickly.

After you send your message, I will read it and try to reply within one week. If I do not know the answer, I will tell you directly instead of guessing.
