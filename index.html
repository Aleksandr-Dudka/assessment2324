About Kharkiv

01Kharkiv In Numbers

Kharkiv is Ukraine’s second-largest city after Kyiv. It is located in the east of the country, near the border with russia. It is home to the largest square in Ukraine. Living in Kharkiv feels very different from living in a compact European city.

02Former Kharkiv

Kharkiv is a former capital that lost its status, but many buildings and places from that time are still there. When you walk through the city centre, you can still feel that Kharkiv had a more important political role in the past. The large buildings like Derzhprom, the wide streets and Freedom Square make the centre feel different from many other cities.

  • 1.4M people
  • 14 years as capital
  • 1654 year founded
  • 350 km² city area

Student life

03Everyday Kharkiv

Kharkiv is also a student city. A lot of students from Ukraine and other countries study there. I studied at NURE, so I know the student atmosphere of the city myself. There are many cafés and places where students meet with friends, and there is also a lot of nightlife.
